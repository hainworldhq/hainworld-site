Hybrid AI Native: Unifying cloud, edge, and agentic intelligence

Why Hybrid AI Native, and why now

AI systems have evolved beyond single deployment models. Cloud, edge, and agentic workflows are now interconnected parts of a single system landscape.

This shift demands clearer language and structure. HAIN World documents this evolution through architecture notes and conceptual assets.
